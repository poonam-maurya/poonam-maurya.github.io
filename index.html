About Me

Welcome! My name is Poonam Maurya, and I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of California, Merced, USA, working on the F3 (Farms, Food and Future) project. Before that, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at Aalborg University, Denmark (2023–2025), working on the PipeSENSE and COMMECT projects. I received my Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Bhilai in 2024 and my B.Tech from the National Institute of Technology Hamirpur in 2017.

My research focuses on the Internet of Things (IoT) and Wireless Communication, especially Low-Power Wide-Area Networks (LPWAN) such as LoRa and LoRaWAN, radio propagation measurements, channel modelling, and data-driven network design for environmental, agricultural, and industrial monitoring. I am also exploring how machine learning can be applied to solve challenging problems in wireless communication.
